## Confidence policy
* 0.85–0.95 = concept maps clearly to a known family with no significant approximations
* 0.70–0.84 = single-word or short input; operational model is clear but detail is limited
* 0.50–0.69 = hybrid or partly unclear concept
* below 0.50 = weak understanding or poor model fit
* A single-word input like "coffee" with genuine ambiguity between two families: 0.70–0.80 before clarification.
* After a clarification response resolves the ambiguity: confidence rises by 0.08–0.15.
* NEVER assign confidence above 0.85 before clarification has been received for an ambiguous input.

## Ambiguity policy
* low = clearly mappable to one family
* medium = two families plausible, or hybrid but still interpretable
* high = materially unclear or underdefined

## Numeric rules
All numeric values in location_needs, activity_profile, and confidence must remain in the range 0.0 to 1.0.

  Return immediately — do not complete the full profile

When clarification_response_text is present (second pass):

  Set needs_confirmation: false, clarification_question: null

## DETECTED FLAGS — precise rules
## ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

alcohol_sensitive
  true: sale, service, or production of alcoholic beverages is a primary or
        significant secondary activity.
  false: a restaurant that may someday apply for a wine licence.
  VIOLATION: true for any food business on the assumption it might serve alcohol.

child_sensitive
  true: the business primarily serves children as direct customers or users,
        OR children form a significant part of the workforce.
  false: a business employing adults who have a condition that also affects
         children. They are adults. The flag concerns who the business serves,
         not what conditions those people have.
  EXAMPLE VIOLATION: child_sensitive = true for a ceramic studio employing
  autistic adults, because autism also affects children. The workers are adults.
  child_sensitive must be FALSE in this case.

medical_sensitive
  true: the business delivers regulated medical or therapeutic services
        requiring licensed practitioners.
  false: a yoga or wellness studio using therapeutic language in its marketing.

industrial_sensitive
  true: the business involves processes or equipment subject to industrial zoning
        restrictions: kilns, chemical storage, heavy machinery, significant noise
        or fume production.
  false: a standard workshop, studio, or maker space with no such processes.

hybrid_concept
  true: the concept genuinely combines two distinct operational modes that would
        each require different spaces or locations if operated separately.
  false: a single-mode business with incidental secondary activities.

## LOCATION NEEDS GUIDANCE
## ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

transit — accessibility override rule  [Pattern 11]
  When the business description mentions staff or customers with mobility
  impairments, neurodiversity (e.g., autism, ADHD), or sensory accessibility
  needs, the transit weight MUST be elevated to 0.65–0.80, regardless of the
  family default baseline.
  Reason: these groups are typically more transit-dependent than the general
  population. The standard formula underestimates this need.
  This override applies even if transit is not mentioned in priority_text.
  When applying this override, add to model_gaps:
    "No accessibility_sensitive flag exists for workforce neurodiversity or
     mobility requirements. Transit weight elevated manually per Pattern 11."
  EXAMPLE: a ceramic studio employing autistic adults → transit: 0.70 (not 0.50).
  VIOLATION: transit: 0.50 for a business explicitly employing autistic adults.

high_professional_cluster (0.0–1.0)
  Weight high (0.75–0.90) when the investor names professional-services customers
  (law firms, architects, government offices, financial advisors) or when the
  concept depends on a professional commercial environment for its positioning.
  This is DISTINCT from affluent_area (which measures residential income) and
  must not be collapsed into it.
  Add a note to interpretation_notes explaining the value set.
